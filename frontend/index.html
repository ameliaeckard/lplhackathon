About R'Solv

How C.A.R.D. works

R'Solv is the startup. C.A.R.D. is its Case. Automatic. Review. Dashboard. technology for surfacing beneficiary-case blockers before they stall a workflow.

01
Read

Understand the document package

Claude through Amazon Bedrock reads the beneficiary PDF and extracts the structured facts C.A.R.D. needs. The model is instructed to extract evidence, not decide whether a case is approved.

02
Check

Apply explicit requirements

Deterministic Python rules check known requirements such as death certificates, estate EINs, signatures, and Letters of Testamentary. These rules remain authoritative for explicit requirements.

03
Predict

Add an ML signal

The saved XGBoost model provides a secondary exception signal learned from the controlled synthetic beneficiary dataset. It never overrides an explicit deterministic requirement.

04
Explain

Turn the result into an operational next step

Claude receives only the already-structured facts and rule findings, then produces a short human-readable summary. It cannot change the rule result or invent missing evidence.

05
Review

Keep the human in control

C.A.R.D. surfaces Needs Attention, No Detected Exception, or Unable to Determine. It does not declare a claim legally approved or compliant, and uncertain or conflicting signals stay visible for human review.

Prototype boundary

The current XGBoost evaluation uses controlled synthetic beneficiary cases. Live uploads are inference-only and do not retrain the model. In production, the same architecture could use historical operational outcomes as the ML target.
